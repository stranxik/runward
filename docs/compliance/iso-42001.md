# Lens: ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system)

The region-agnostic anchor. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is a **certifiable** AI management system (AIMS) standard, verified by an accredited third party. It is process/organisational, global, and offends no market — the best default when you don't want to name a jurisdiction.

**What ISO 42001 asks for (relevant here):** documented information and records — an AI policy, an inventory, risk & impact assessments, model-governance documentation, operational records, monitoring evidence, and an audit trail across the AI lifecycle (clauses 4–10, notably 7.5 documented information).

## How runward's output maps

| ISO 42001 expectation | runward artifact |
|---|---|
| Documented risk identification / controls | The `Rule conformance` manifest: each CRITICAL/HIGH craft rule accounted for (`applied` with a `file:line`/test, `deviated` with an ADR, or `n/a` with a reason). |
| Traceable decisions & records | The ADR journal — every structural decision with context, alternatives, and a dated re-evaluation trigger (retro-reconstructed via `characterize` for existing systems). |
| Agentic risk coverage | The OWASP ASI01–ASI10 mapping on the rules, readable as security-risk coverage. |
| Operational / oversight records | The threat model, evaluation rubric, observability schema, and runbook scaffolded by `runward init`. |
| Handover / accountability | The `AGENTS.md` charter and the transmission kit. |

## Honest boundary

runward gives you **audit-ready supporting evidence** and traceability artifacts that an AIMS programme can use as inputs. It does **not** make an organisation "ISO 42001 compliant" or certified — only an accredited certification body certifies, and only the organisation holds the AIMS. Verify the current standard text before an audit.
